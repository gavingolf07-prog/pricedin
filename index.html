Weighted-average diluted shares, in millions, from the reported income statements. A falling share count (buybacks) lifts EPS; a rising one dilutes it. Shares are not projected, because EPS growth is entered directly.

How the numbers work
  • Projected price = projected EPS × the P/E you expect at the end of that year.
  • Annualized return compares the projected price with today's price over the years since your last reported fiscal year. Dividends are not included.
  • Buy at or below is the final-year projected price discounted at your target return. Use the 3, 4 or 5 year switch to set how far out that final year is.
  • Projected revenue grows from your last reported revenue at the revenue growth rate you set for each case. It does not affect the price projection, which comes from EPS × P/E.

  • Edit any shaded cell. Type EPS or growth in a projected year and the other one follows. Each company tab is saved separately, in this browser only. Double-click a tab to rename it.
